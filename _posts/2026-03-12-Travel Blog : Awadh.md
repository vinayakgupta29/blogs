A fantastic trip came to my mind on days prior I wanted to visit awadh. I planned initially to do it on weekends. As recently I've got a job that had me travelling places. And because of which I was in Barabanki UP,  which is 140 ish km from Awadh. 
 But on 10th my senior said that he'll have to visit Rudrapur in Uttarakhand, so it'll be me and another junior who'll have to take the work. That junior in question has grown fond of me in just the past 5 days that I've worked with him. But he suggested that we can complete other days work on days before so that we can have a day of buffer where we can go to ayodhya. 
  But due to the way our work is structured at the moment he decided to stay and let me go on a solo trip. So, it was decided that he'll handle the work on 12th and I'll go around and enjoy awadh. I guess it was prabhu iccha, cause, Hoihe ji soi ram rachi rakha kau kari tark badhawe sakha,...
   Anyways, I've decided to go to awadh early in the morning like 5:30 - 6 AM but I slept at 1 am and probably slept through my early morning 4:30 alarm. And woke up at 7 am. 
  Quickly then I finished my daily routine and got ready and got on a bus at around 9 for awadh got to awadh around 11.
  There the bus dropped me on Awadh -Basti Bypass and I had a cup of Sugarcane juice. Then I asked a few auto walas to get me to Hanuman Gadhi temple, the price they stated was 100 per person. As I was a single traveller that's too much... I walked like a km or so and then asked another auto wala and he took me right at the temple maybe like 200 m away cause of crowd management and I just payed him ₹20. Then I bought 2 malas for ₹20 each to be offered to the lord inside. 
  After like 20 mins in the queue I got to the temple it has 3 different doors to the garbhhagriha and I got the one which is on the left hand side of the diety. I got good darshan of Shri Ram there but not of the Hanuman there... I was just able to glance at the Mukut or the Gadi of Hanuman. Even the mala that I gave to the pujari he took it and just put it outside. Didn't even put it on the lord's feet at all. Then on the exit route I saw a place where people were singing bhajans and it also had an led screen there showing the darshan inside. The moment I came to the screen it closed. And then I moved to the Sadhu Gadi there and changed Jai Ram shree ram jai jai ram 108 times and a round of Hanuman chalisa. From there many people can have the darshan of Hanuman but not me... Then when I decided to go out I the LED screen was still closed off. So I moved on for dashrath Bhawan.  And there I didn't bought any more puja samagri to offer other than the cash in my pocket cause what's the point of they aren't gonna even serve it to the lord. 

    But sadly that wasn't the case in other temples like Dashrath bhawan and Kanak bhawan I could've offered a mala to the lord but I guess it was not in my bhagya to receive such blessings. But the darshan at these temples were fantastic and I'll go over to them in a bit...

So after hanuman gadhi I went to the Dashrath Bhawan, temple. At the time of arrival the darshan was closed due to Bhog. I stopped a while to wait for darshan. There in the temple complex I saw a priest taking donations for a Gaushala and there I gave them a ₹50 and sat besides him. He asked me where I was from and did I had lunch or not. Then he offered me to have lunch here at the temple. So I agreed. A few mins later Darshan opened up with such low crowd the Darshan was ecclesiastical and other-wordly. 
    After Darshan I went in for prasadam and I had 2 roti, some aaloo ki sabzi and some rice and some dal. now the thing is I am on travel on my own so I didn't went up and ate a stomach full cause I still had to walk and not sleep.
    After the Dashrath Bhavan I moved to the Main "Shri Raam JanmaBhoomi Temple" and going in I was very emotional and religiously motivated and like I was practically crying as I was stepping in and singing *"Shri Ram Stuti"* but the thing is due to the heat and internal crowd missmanagement I wasn't able to gaze at the architecture nor at the Lord's Vigraha. 
        Like in person at close the time was very little to even pay attention to his Swaroopa and all this time I was glued to the LED screens they had as the lines were moving slowly but I think they could've done a better Job with crowd management but it's india so what can we expect. 
        After coming out I ate Khichadi and drank an ORS tera-pack at Sita Rasoi and moved on for Kanak Bhavan. 
    The Kanak Bhavan darshan to open up as it was nearby... After a while I moved out for *Kanak Bhavan* and then it was the darshan at kanak bhawan... there were still a few minutes till the darshan so I waited there and people were chanting lord Sita-Ram's names and there was a kirtan so I was out there with them enjoying that kirtan... 
    After a while the darshan opened up and the crowd rushed in. I stayed back a little while so that I could have the Darshan in peace. And then I went in the line the Darshan was so ecclesiastical that I haven't seen anywhere else. The fact of the matter was the Pose of the Lord. In almost all Ram Temples across India Shri Ram is standing with Maa Sita always. But here I saw them sitting like sitting in Padmasana like sitting on a throne or like sitting in a resting pose.  
    The legend was that the Kanak Bhavan was Lord's `Ranga-Bhavan` i.e his resting home. 
    Returning from the Kanak Bhavan Temple I found a small road side temple with an old priest and there I felt like giving so I donated a Rs. 50 in his collection bowl. Then he started mentioning some Chaupai (Verses) from Ramcharitmanas which I don't reckon for now but the jist was that "Whatever happens, happens by the will of the Lord... Good or Bad, Harm or Help... But one must persists in his own doings... Fruits will be given by the Lord himself..."
 Anyways I moved to the Saryu Ghat, the local auto wala's were asking 200 for 2km ride so. I took out a little detour to the Swaminarayana Temple in Ayodhya and used their premises for changing back into my formal pants from Dhoti. But the temple was in so much obscurity and like random streets and all without any markings at all.

 Afterwards I moved towards the Saryu Ghat for Saryu Aarti and there was again the same 200 for the 2 km ride. But a kind Shikanji seller told me to get a Government taxi service for just Rs. 20 instead. So I boarded that and on the Ghat went on for the Aarti... now there were multiple ghats and each had their own timings for Aarti so I was a bit confused and tired.
    So I went to pakka ghat and did the aachman and then just put my feet in the water to chill off. after a while when I felt like I was getting late for my return journey I decied to get on moving and on my way back I found a priest conducting Saryu Aarti so I stood there and paid my penance. There I noticed some normal non-priest people also performing the Aarti but I thought they might be the priest's Yajman and they've paid for it. So I just stood there but then a saw a random passer by also performing the same. So I asked just went in and performed the aarti untill i accidentally burned my finger with that flame. But then I saw someone making a video of some other person performing the Aarti. So I gave him my phone and asked to record the video.   
    I got the video done and after the Aarti, I talked with the priest a little and since I wasn't able to get a returning train the same day so they told me how can I get a bus way back to Barabanki. and I did as they said and I got to Barabanki by 11 PM in the night and surprisingly in the UP I didn't got robbed or murdered even this late in Night. 

    So the conclusion of the whole trip was very divine and ecclesiastical for me and the randomness of Blissful events that happened to me that were not on my plan list, like, The Prasadam at Dashrath Bhavan, the Darshan at Kanak Bhavan, The random Priest's Blessings and the Saryu Aarti.
